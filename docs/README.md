# LexFlow: Law Firm Case Management Portal

LexFlow is a secure, web-based case management portal for law firms. It centralizes cases, clients, documents, deadlines, and tasks that are often scattered across files, emails, and spreadsheets, making it easier for legal teams to find information, track progress, coordinate work, and protect confidential data.

## Features
- Secure authentication and **role-based access control** so attorneys and staff can only view or modify information appropriate to their responsibilities. 
- Users can create and update case records, manage client information, upload and organize documents, assign tasks, and monitor key deadlines. 
- Activity logging to record actions such as creating, editing, viewing, or deleting case information for accountability and auditing.

## Current Goal
We want to produce a functional prototype that demonstrates how a centralized information system can improve efficiency, collaboration, security, and accountability in a real-world law firm setting. The design also lays groundwork for future enhancements such as client access, automated reminders, calendar integration, electronic signatures, reporting tools, and cloud deployment.
